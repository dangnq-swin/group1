Our mission and vision

We aim for a better place where people can have access to education that fits their wallet. That's why we put money where our mouth is: at only large and private educational facilities. We hope that by providing our expertise to only the best and most privileged students, we will make the world better as a whole.

Mission

In practice: every release is tested with screen readers, keyboard-only navigation, and users with low vision before it ships.

Access first

Every product ships meeting WCAG 2.2 AA, and we test with assistive technology before release.

Open by default

Our design system and accessibility checklists are published, so other small teams can reuse them.

Vision

Where we are heading: a platform that adapts to the learner rather than asking the learner to adapt to it.

Low bandwidth

Pages are built to work on a 3G connection, because students are not always on campus wifi.

Local partnerships

We work with community education providers to keep course content affordable and relevant.
